              Frequently asked

              How do I raise an RFI on a project?

              Open the project, go to the RFIs tab, and use the Respond action on an open RFI. New RFIs come in from consultants and show in your dashboard's Pending RFIs table.

              Who can see the tasks I create?

              Tasks are visible to everyone on the project team. The assignee and the person who assigned it get notified based on their notification preferences.

              How do I record minutes for a meeting?

              Hover a meeting in Your Schedule and choose Notes. Saved minutes appear in the MOM Tracker under Meetings.

              Can I switch between roles?

              Yes. Use the role switcher under your name in the top bar. The role you pick changes which approvals and actions you see.

              Where do approved makes and standard drawings live?

              In the Library. Each tab has its own register with search and discipline filters.
